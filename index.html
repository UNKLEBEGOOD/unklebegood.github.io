Featured Projects

Healthcare Claims & Billing Analysis

Analyzed 70,000 billing records across 7 insurance payers to uncover where revenue was being lost in the claims cycle. Built an end-to-end pipeline in Python (pandas) and SQL Server (T-SQL, cross-validated), then designed a Power BI dashboard to track denials and collections. Found a $40M revenue gap (64.5% collection rate) driven mainly by preventable submission errors — duplicate claims and missing prior authorizations — rather than payer-specific issues.

Social Media Impact on Student Life

Analyzed 4,500 student records as a data analyst consultant for the Ministry of Information and Communication Technology, examining how daily social media usage relates to sleep, academic performance, stress, and mental health. Found that total daily usage — not age, gender, platform, or academic level — was the strongest driver of outcomes, correlating with mental health (−0.85), stress (+0.75), sleep (−0.72), and GPA (−0.70).
